SD-02 — Connect Same BGM/Chapter-Based BGM. [P1]

VERSION 1 RULE: Same BGM and Chapter-Based BGM are mutually exclusive
strategies. They must not play or mix two BGM tracks side by side. Use
one selected BGM track at a time for the relevant scope. Advanced layered
or simultaneous multi-track BGM mixing is explicitly deferred until after
Version 1 is complete.

Same BGM:
- The user selects one book-default BGM track for the entire story.
- That same track is used throughout every chapter while BGM is enabled.
- No chapter-specific BGM overrides are used in this mode.
- The book-level master BGM volume applies to the selected track.
- Preview and final audiobook generation use the same saved track and
  master-volume settings.
- Opening, Next Chapter and Closing cues remain separate cue assignments,
  governed by the global BGM/cue On/Off setting.

Chapter-Based BGM:
- When this strategy is selected, the book-default BGM is ignored entirely
  for generation. It must not be mixed underneath or automatically applied
  to any chapter.
- Each chapter can have at most one explicitly selected BGM track in
  Version 1, or no BGM.
- The user must explicitly select and save a track for each chapter where
  background music is wanted.
- A chapter without an explicit saved track assignment has no BGM. Do not
  fall back silently to the book-default track.
- Per-chapter volume, start offset and end/fade behaviour apply to that
  chapter's selected track and must affect the actual audio.
- Preview and final generation use the same saved chapter assignments and
  their volume/timing settings.
- Reusing the same track in several chapters is permitted, but the system
  must not layer it with the ignored book-default track.
- Switching between strategies must preserve saved configuration safely.
  Returning to Same BGM uses the saved book-default track; returning to
  Chapter-Based BGM uses only the explicitly saved chapter assignments.

Both strategies:
- Global Voice Only / BGM Off overrides the selected strategy and excludes
  all background music and configured Opening/Next Chapter/Closing cues.
- When BGM/cues are enabled, the fixed cue assignments remain separate from
  background music; they are not additional BGM tracks.
- Do not provide per-chapter Use Book Default as an assignment option in
  Chapter-Based BGM. The allowed chapter choices are one explicitly selected
  custom track or No BGM.
- Do not require side-by-side BGM input, multi-layer composition, automatic
  default-track fallback or complex cross-track orchestration in Version 1.
- Advanced layered BGM and multi-track mixing may be considered for a future
  version only after Version 1 is completed.

This choice must update the Mix & Production Sync summary and Production
Studio's generation settings. The selected strategy and its exact effective
tracks must be visible in the current project state.
